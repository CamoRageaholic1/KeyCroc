## Federal Laws (United States)

### Computer Fraud and Abuse Act (CFAA)
**18 U.S.C. § 1030**

**Prohibits:**
- Accessing a computer without authorization
- Exceeding authorized access
- Obtaining information from protected computers
- Causing damage to protected computers

**Penalties:**
- First offense: Up to 5 years imprisonment
- Repeat offenses: Up to 10 years imprisonment
- Fines up to $250,000
- Civil liability for damages

### Electronic Communications Privacy Act (ECPA)
**18 U.S.C. § 2510-2523**

**Prohibits:**
- Intercepting electronic communications
- Accessing stored communications without authorization
- Using information obtained illegally

**Penalties:**
- Up to 5 years imprisonment
- Fines
- Civil damages

### Wiretap Act
**18 U.S.C. § 2511**

**Prohibits:**
- Intercepting wire, oral, or electronic communications
- Disclosing intercepted communications

**Penalties:**
- Up to 5 years imprisonment
- Fines up to $250,000
